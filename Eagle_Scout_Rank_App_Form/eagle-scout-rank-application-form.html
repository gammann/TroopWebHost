Application form source

512-728 — Eagle Scout Rank Application

This tool fills whatever PDF is at the link below. Scouting America updates this form occasionally — if the link ever breaks, get the current URL from scouting.org's Eagle Scout awards page, paste it in here, and click Save. This is stored in the browser (not in the page code), so any admin of this custom page can update it without editing anything.

Step 4

Positions of responsibility & remaining requirements

Requirement 4 only counts time served after the Life board of review. Positions that overlap that date are listed below with dates pre-adjusted - check the ones to include (you can list up to two) and edit dates if needed.

Step 5

Generate & download

This fills the official BSA form 512-728 (Jan 2026 revision) client-side, in your browser - nothing is uploaded anywhere. Still needed by hand: references (Requirement 2), the ambitions/leadership statement attachment, and all signatures.
