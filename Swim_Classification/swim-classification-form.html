The supervisor sign-off block at the bottom of the form is left blank for the test supervisor to fill by hand -- TroopWebHost has no record of that. Rosters over 15 people generate extra copies of the blank form page, filled 15 at a time, with the procedures page attached once at the end. A red "expired" flag on a Test Date means it will be more than a year old by the last day of this campout -- BSA requires swim classification to be renewed annually; that person likely needs a retest before this trip. Medical Recheck shows the earlier of TroopWebHost's Medical Part A/B dates and is flagged (red, "expired") once the last day of the month, 12 months after that date, will have passed by the last day of this campout, or flagged ("missing") if there's no Part A/B date on file at all -- it's read-only here (nothing is saved back for it). "Add Scout"/"Add Adult" pulls from TroopWebHost's own roster and fills in current Test Date/Swim Level/Medical Recheck automatically. "Save to TroopWebHost" writes Test Date / Swim Level corrections back to TroopWebHost's own swim classification records for each matched person -- a real database change, applied to everyone listed here regardless of whether their checkbox is ticked (the checkbox only controls who's on the generated PDF).
